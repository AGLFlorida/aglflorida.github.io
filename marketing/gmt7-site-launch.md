## LinkedIn
New site launch: George Merrick Troop 7 of Coral Gables.

AGL Consulting designed and built the website for George Merrick Troop 7 of Coral Gables, Inc, the nonprofit that charters Troop 7, Troop 719, Pack 7, and Crew 7, and holds the deed to the historic Troop 7 Scouting cabin in Coral Gables.

The site covers:
- Mission, vision, and values for the chartering organization
- Links out to each unit (Troop 7, Troop 719, Pack 7, Crew 7)
- Cabin location and lease details with the City of Coral Gables
- A full Spanish-language version

Our founder also serves as the organization's board president, so this one was personal as well as professional.

If your nonprofit or civic organization needs a site that is clear, bilingual, and easy to maintain, that is exactly the kind of work we do.
