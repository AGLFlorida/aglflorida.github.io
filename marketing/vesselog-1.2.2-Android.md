## LinkedIn
VesseLog is live on Android: our vessel maintenance app for boat owners and anyone keeping a small fleet on the water.

A weekend on the water shouldn't start with wondering when the impeller was last replaced. VesseLog keeps it all in one place:

- Smart scheduling: a calendar of every maintenance task across your fleet
- Recurring tasks: set an interval once and the next one schedules itself
- Service history: a searchable record of completed work for every vessel
- Vendor management: providers, contact info, and specialties, organized
- Privacy by default: attach a photo from your gallery without granting access to your whole library

We built it with crew coaching staffs in mind, too. Safety and coaching launches rack up engine hours fast, and VesseLog tracks oil changes, prop checks, and engine service across the whole fleet so no boat is sidelined on race day.

Plans start at up to 3 boats, with fleet pricing for larger operations and marine service businesses. Reach out and we'll get you set up:
