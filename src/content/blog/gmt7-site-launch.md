## A New Home Online for George Merrick Troop 7, Inc., a 501(c)(3)

**October 15, 2026** — We designed and built the new website for [George Merrick Troop 7 of Coral Gables, Inc.](https://gmt7.org/), a Florida not-for-profit that serves as the chartering organization for Troop 7, Troop 719, Pack 7, and Crew 7. The organization owns the deed to the historic Troop 7 Cabin at 1107 South Greenway Drive in Coral Gables, manages the cabin's long-term lease with the City of Coral Gables, and funds the units that meet there.

## The Problem

A chartering organization like this one sits behind the scenes of the Scouting units everyone actually sees. Before this project, there was no single place for parents, city officials, or prospective members to find the organization's mission, its board, or how the historic cabin fits into the picture. The site needed to explain all of that clearly, in both English and Spanish, and stay easy for a volunteer board to maintain going forward.

## What We Built

The new site covers:

- **Mission, Vision, and Values** — plain statements of the organization's purpose: youth leadership, service, intergenerational fellowship, civic engagement with local government, and stewardship of the cabin.
- **Unit Directory** — direct links out to the websites for Troop 7, Troop 719, Pack 7, and Crew 7, so visitors land on the right page for the group they're looking for.
- **Cabin and Location** — the cabin's address and a map link, plus the organization's relationship with the City of Coral Gables.
- **Board and Contact** — who runs the organization and how to reach them.
- **Full Spanish-Language Version** — every page available in Spanish, not a partial translation bolted on after the fact.

## Why We Built It

Our founder also serves as the organization's board president, so this engagement started from direct, first-hand familiarity with what the board and the Scouting families actually needed from the site, not a cold brief. That is the same approach we bring to any client project: understand how the organization actually operates before writing a line of code.
