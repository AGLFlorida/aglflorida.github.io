# VesseLog Launches on iOS

**October 8, 2026** — AGL Consulting is proud to announce the launch of **VesseLog** on iOS, a vessel maintenance app for boat owners and anyone keeping a small fleet on the water.

A weekend on the water shouldn't start with wondering when the impeller was last replaced. VesseLog keeps it all in one place.

## Key Features

- **Smart Scheduling**
  A calendar of every maintenance task across your fleet.

- **Recurring Tasks**
  Set an interval once and the next one schedules itself.

- **Service History**
  A searchable record of completed work for every vessel.

- **Vendor Management**
  Providers, contact info, and specialties, organized.

## Built for Fleets, Not Just One Boat

We built VesseLog with crew coaching staffs in mind, too. Safety and coaching launches rack up engine hours fast, and VesseLog tracks oil changes, prop checks, and engine service across the whole fleet so no boat is sidelined on race day.

## Why Maintenance Tracking Breaks Down

Most fleets we talked to were running on some mix of a paper logbook, a shared spreadsheet, and whatever the previous owner scrawled on a sticker in the engine bay. That works until the person who knows the schedule is on vacation, or the spreadsheet gets edited on two phones at once, or a sticker gets replaced and the history with it. None of those failures show up until a part goes past due and nobody notices in time.

VesseLog is built around the idea that maintenance should be a system, not a memory. Every task lives on a shared calendar instead of in one person's head, every completed job writes itself into a permanent history instead of a sticky note, and every vendor relationship is attached to the vessel it serves instead of a separate contacts app. The goal is that a new crew member, a new captain, or a new owner can open the app and immediately know what's been done and what's next.
