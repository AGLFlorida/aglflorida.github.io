# VesseLog Launches on Android

**October 22, 2026** — AGL Consulting is proud to announce the launch of **VesseLog** on Android, a vessel maintenance app for boat owners and anyone keeping a small fleet on the water.

A weekend on the water shouldn't start with wondering when the impeller was last replaced. VesseLog keeps it all in one place.

## Key Features

- **Smart Scheduling**
  A calendar of every maintenance task across your fleet.

- **Recurring Tasks**
  Set an interval once and the next one schedules itself.

- **Service History**
  A searchable record of completed work for every vessel.

- **Vendor Management**
  Providers, contact info, and specialties, organized.

- **Privacy by Default**
  Attach a photo from your gallery without granting access to your whole library.

## Built for Fleets, Not Just One Boat

We built VesseLog with crew coaching staffs in mind, too. Safety and coaching launches rack up engine hours fast, and VesseLog tracks oil changes, prop checks, and engine service across the whole fleet so no boat is sidelined on race day.

## Why Privacy by Default Matters

Attaching a photo to a maintenance record, a cracked hose, a corroded fitting, the part number stamped on an impeller, is one of the most common things people do in VesseLog. Most apps ask for blanket access to your entire photo library just to let you pick one image, which means granting a maintenance app permission to see every photo on your phone, vacation pictures included.

On Android, VesseLog uses the system photo picker instead. You choose the one photo you want to attach, and that's the only photo the app ever sees. No broad gallery permission, no standing access you have to remember to revoke later. It's a small technical difference that adds up to a real one: the app only knows what you show it.
