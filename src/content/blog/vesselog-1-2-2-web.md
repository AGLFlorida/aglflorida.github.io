# VesseLog Is Now on the Web

**October 29, 2026** — VesseLog now runs in your browser. Sign in from any computer and your whole fleet's maintenance is right there, no install required.

Not every maintenance job gets planned from a phone. The coaching staff building next week's launch schedule, or the owner lining up winter work with the yard, usually has a laptop open. So we put the full app in the browser.

## What You Get

- The same maintenance calendar, service history, and vendor contacts as the mobile app
- One account across your phone and your computer
- Works on desktop, tablet, and phone screens

## Why It Works This Way

For us this was a build-once decision: the web version runs the same screens as the mobile app, so a fix or a feature lands everywhere at once. We didn't want to maintain two different products that happened to share a name, where a calendar bug fixed on mobile quietly stayed broken on the web, or a new feature shipped to phones first and the browser months later. One codebase, one set of screens, every platform gets the same app at the same time.

## Who This Is For

Mobile is where VesseLog shines for quick updates: logging a completed task from the dock, snapping a photo of a part before you order a replacement, checking tomorrow's schedule from your pocket. But planning tends to happen somewhere else. A coaching staff building out a season's worth of launch maintenance, a marina office reconciling vendor invoices, an owner comparing service history across a whole fleet before a survey, that's spreadsheet-and-second-monitor work, and it's easier with a keyboard and a bigger screen.

The web app isn't a stripped-down companion to the mobile apps. It's the same application, running the same data, so whichever device you reach for, you're looking at the current state of your fleet, not a cached or partial view of it.
