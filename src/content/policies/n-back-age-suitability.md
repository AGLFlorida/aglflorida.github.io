# Age Suitability for Dual N-Back

Last updated: 02-Sep-2026

## Overview
Dual N-Back is a cognitive training game built by AGL Consulting. This page describes the app's content and data practices as they relate to age suitability, for reference alongside the app's listing on the App Store and Google Play.

## Content Rating
Dual N-Back contains no violence, mature themes, user-generated content, or social features. Gameplay consists of tracking sequences of spoken letters and grid positions. There is no chat, messaging, or way for users to interact with one another.

## Advertising
Dual N-Back does not display advertising.

## In-App Purchases
Dual N-Back is free and does not offer in-app purchases.

## Data Collection
Dual N-Back does not collect personal information. Game settings and performance data are stored locally on the device. See our [Privacy Policy](/policies/n-back-policy) for details.

## Suitability
Dual N-Back is suitable for all ages, including children, as a memory and focus training tool. There is no content requiring an age restriction.
