# Age Suitability for RecallKit

Last updated: 02-Sep-2026

## Overview
RecallKit is a flashcard study app built by AGL Consulting. This page describes the app's content and data practices as they relate to age suitability, for reference alongside the app's listing on the App Store.

## Content Rating
RecallKit contains no violence, mature themes, or social features. Flashcard decks are created and used privately by the individual user; there is no chat, messaging, or sharing between users within the app.

## Advertising
RecallKit displays third-party video ads served through networks such as Google AdMob. These ad networks may collect limited device and usage information to serve and measure ads, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/policies/recall-kit-policy). Ad content is subject to the ad network's own content and audience controls.

## In-App Purchases
RecallKit offers optional in-app purchases to remove ads and unlock additional features, managed through the App Store billing system.

## Data Collection
RecallKit does not collect personal information such as name, email, or location. See our [Privacy Policy](/policies/recall-kit-policy) for details, including information about advertising identifiers collected by third-party ad networks.

## Suitability
RecallKit is a study and learning tool suitable for students and general audiences. Because it displays third-party advertising, App Store and Google Play age ratings should reflect the presence of ads. There is no other content requiring an age restriction.
