# Privacy Policy for Tellurion

Last updated: 17-Aug-2026

## Introduction
AGL Consulting ("we" or "us" or "our") respects the privacy of our users ("user" or "you").
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## Information We Collect
The App collects the following data:
- App settings and preferences
- Log entries and data you create within the App
- Usage and interaction data (via Firebase Analytics)
- Device and crash diagnostic data (via Firebase Crashlytics)

## How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Save your entries, progress, and settings
- Understand how the App is used so we can improve features and performance
- Identify and fix crashes and stability issues
- Provide customer support

## Third-Party Services
The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information used to identify you:
- **Firebase Analytics** (Google) — collects anonymized usage and interaction data.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google) — collects crash reports and device diagnostic data.

These services may transmit data to Google's servers for processing. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details on how Google handles this data.

## Data Storage and Security
Your log entries and settings are stored locally on your device. Usage analytics and crash diagnostic data are transmitted to Firebase (Google) as described above. We do not sell your personal data.

## User Rights
You can:
- Delete all app data by uninstalling the app
- Contact us at [support@aglflorida.com] for support

## Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
