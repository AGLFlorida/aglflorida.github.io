# Age Suitability for VesseLog

Last updated: 02-Sep-2026

## Overview
VesseLog is a water craft maintenance companion app built by AGL Consulting for boat owners and marine professionals. This page describes the app's content and data practices as they relate to age suitability, for reference alongside the app's listing on the App Store and Google Play.

## Content Rating
VesseLog contains no violence, mature themes, or social features. The app is a maintenance scheduling and record-keeping tool. Any content within the app (log entries, notes, vendor information) is entered by the user for their own use and is not shared publicly or with other users.

## Advertising
VesseLog does not display advertising.

## In-App Purchases
VesseLog is a subscription based service, managed directly with the AGL sales team.

## Data Collection
VesseLog stores log entries and settings locally on the device, and uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand usage and diagnose crashes. See our [Privacy Policy](/policies/vesselog-policy) for details.

## Suitability
VesseLog is a professional and personal maintenance tool intended for general audiences, including boat owners and marine professionals of any age. There is no content requiring an age restriction.
