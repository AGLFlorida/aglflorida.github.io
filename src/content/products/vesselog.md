VesseLog helps boat owners and marine professionals manage vessel maintenance schedules, track service history, and stay on top of upcoming tasks.

Visit [vesselog.com](https://vesselog.com) for pricing and details.

## Examples

**Crew Coaching Staff**
Coaches run their motorized safety and coaching launches hard — on the water every practice, every regatta, all season long. Between chase-boat duty and towing, engines rack up hours fast. With VesseLog, the coaching staff logs oil changes, prop checks, and engine service across the whole fleet of launches in one place, so no boat gets left waiting on a broken part on race day.

**Boat Owners**
A weekend on the water shouldn't start with wondering when the impeller was last replaced. Boat owners use VesseLog to keep every service record, vendor contact, and upcoming maintenance task for their vessel in one app — so the only thing left to plan is where to go.
